SUPPORT

Need help? Email tejash.apps@gmail.com. We reply as soon as we can, usually within a day or two. Please include your gamertag.

HOW DO I DELETE MY ACCOUNT?

Open the Lobby, walk to (or tap) the Settings door, then tap Delete Account. This removes your drops, reactions, friend records, messages and parties you own. It cannot be undone.

HOW DO I REPORT OR BLOCK SOMEONE?

On a drop, tap ⋯ and choose Report drop or Block player. In a chat or party, press and hold a message to report it, or use the ⋯ menu in a 1-to-1 chat to block the player. Reported drops disappear for you right away.

I CAN'T SEE MY FRIEND'S DROPS OR MESSAGES.

Check that you're signed in to iCloud in the iOS Settings app and that you have a connection. Pull down on the Arena or the Friends list to refresh. If you blocked this person earlier, they stay hidden.

A FRIEND REQUEST ISN'T SHOWING UP.

Open Chats, go to Requests, and pull down to refresh. Gamertags must be spelled exactly (capital letters don't matter).

CAN I CHANGE MY GAMERTAG?

Not yet. For now, delete your account and set up again, or email us and we'll help.

HOW DO I REPORT A PROBLEM OR A SAFETY CONCERN?

Use Report in the app, or email tejash.apps@gmail.com. If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.
