PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 2, 2026
The short version: Respawn lets you share gaming screenshots and chat with friends. We store what you choose to share so the app can work. We don't run ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your data. You can delete your account and your data inside the app at any time.

WHO WE ARE

Respawn is an iPhone app published by Shrika LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who can see it, and what choices you have. Questions: tejash.apps@gmail.com.

WHAT WE COLLECT AND WHY

  • Gamertag. The name you choose when you start. Other players can see it next to your drops and use it to find you. Purpose: to show who posted and let friends find you.
  • Drops you post. A screenshot you pick from your photo library, the game name, an optional caption, and whether it is an Achievement Unlocked or an Epic Fail. Drops are public to other Respawn players in the Arena. Purpose: to show your drops and let others react.
  • Reactions. When you tap an emoji reaction, it is saved with your account so counts are correct and you can undo it.
  • Friends and Parties. Friend requests, accepted friendships, and party memberships are saved so the app can show your friends and parties. Purpose: to run Chats and Parties.
  • Messages. The messages you send in 1-to-1 chats and parties, with the time and your gamertag. Purpose: to deliver the messages.
  • Account identifier. Respawn uses your iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit. We receive an app-specific identifier tied to it. We never see your Apple ID password.
  • Safety information. When you report a drop or a message, we save the report (what was reported, who sent it, your reason, and who reported it) so we can review it. The list of players you block stays on your device.

Respawn does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone. It reads a photo only when you choose one with the photo picker.

WHERE IT IS STORED

Your data is stored in Apple's CloudKit (the iCloud servers Apple provides to apps). Apple processes it for us under Apple's own privacy terms. Messages are stored in a shared (public) CloudKit database and are shown in the app only to the people in that chat or party, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. Please treat Respawn chat as casual game chat and do not send passwords, financial details or other sensitive information.

WHO CAN SEE WHAT

  • Drops, your gamertag, and reactions: other Respawn players.
  • Messages: the people in that chat or party. We may read a message only if it is reported to us.
  • Reports: only us.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting your information.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

YOUR CHOICES

  • Delete your account and data: in the app, open the Lobby, go to Settings, then tap Delete Account. This removes your drops, reactions, friend records, messages, parties you own, and your directory entry, and clears the data on your device.
  • Delete one drop: open the ⋯ menu on your own drop and choose Delete.
  • Block or report: use the ⋯ menu on any drop, or press and hold a message.
  • Photos: you can change photo access any time in iOS Settings.

Records that other people created, for example a friend request someone sent to you, belong to them and stay until they remove it. If something you need removed is not covered, email us and we will help.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

We keep your information until you delete it or delete your account. Reports are kept as long as needed to keep the community safe.

CHILDREN

Respawn is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using Respawn, email us and we will remove the account.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

CONTACT

Email tejash.apps@gmail.com for questions, data requests, or to report a problem.
